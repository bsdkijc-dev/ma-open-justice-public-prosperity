# Research Ethics Protocol

- **Human Review of Legal Conclusions:** Automated extractions and legal summaries require human legal verification before publication.
- **Vulnerable Populations:** Data involving rural communities, informal workers, micro-enterprises, women, older persons, and persons with disabilities must uphold dignity, non-stigmatization, and strict anonymization.
- **Causal Restraint:** Do not claim causal impact from cross-sectional correlations or output counts alone.
