# Program Charter (2027–2030)

## Mission
Transform verified evidence from six independent access-to-justice observatories into:
1. Comparable research indicators.
2. Cross-project analytical insights.
3. Evidence-based policy recommendations.
4. Public accountability resources.
5. Reproducible research datasets.
6. Practical access-to-justice interventions.
7. Public knowledge products.
8. Measurable public value assessments.

## Central Policy Propositions (Hypotheses to Investigate)
- Judicial transparency can support public trust.
- Accessible justice can reduce barriers to exercising rights.
- Legal certainty can support economic and social security.
- Accessible legal knowledge can improve people's ability to navigate institutions.

*These are empirical propositions to test, never automatic causal conclusions.*

## 12 Core Integration Principles
1. Evidence before conclusions.
2. Official sources before secondary sources.
3. Source traceability for every material claim.
4. Comparable definitions before comparison.
5. Privacy and data minimization.
6. Independent validation.
7. Human review of legal conclusions.
8. Reproducibility.
9. Transparent uncertainty.
10. No unsupported causal claims.
11. No unauthorized institutional representation.
12. Public value rather than publication volume.
