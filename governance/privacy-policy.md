# Privacy & Data Minimization Policy

Never publish or ingest into the public integration layer:
- Identifiable personal information (PII: names, NIK, addresses, phone numbers, emails).
- Restricted case materials or sealed court records.
- Confidential institutional information.
- Unapproved interview or survey microdata.
- Unreviewed legal interpretations.

All integrated records must pass automated PII screening and human review before promotion beyond `P3`/`P5`.
